Regression models on INRIX probe, crash, and roadway data to identify which travel-time performance metrics are most predictive of crash risk at the segment level — early-warning signal design for a safety-critical outcome across work-zone and non-work-zone conditions.

  • Evaluated Travel Time Index, Speed Deficit, and Congestion/Queue Mile-Hour
  • Separated genuine risk drivers from correlated noise
  • Segment-level early-warning signals for safety
