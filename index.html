About Me

I'm a data and applied scientist with 5+ years building machine learning, LLM, and geospatial solutions for transportation agencies, and packaging them as reusable, documented workflows. I've designed a GPT-4 evaluation pipeline validated against behavior from 378,000+ connected vehicles, and built reproducible, AI-assisted analytics workflows (Python, Jupyter, GeoPandas) that carry a project from raw telematics all the way to dashboards, GIS layers, and stakeholder briefings. A Ph.D. in transportation engineering lets me translate AI capabilities into practical solutions for planners, engineers, and clients.
